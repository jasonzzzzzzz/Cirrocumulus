## Findings

**1. LongBench v2 cannot separate the arms.** Units are 40 per model. FP's forced-choice accuracy is
0.425 (Llama) and 0.45 (Qwen); the closed-book arm reaches 0.35 and 0.225. Only 6 (Llama) and 11
(Qwen) units need the context.
- Every arm, including the closed book on Llama, is within ±0.1 of FP.
- No accuracy label is ACC_LOSS.
- The system's NLL is NEAR_FP on Llama and INCONCLUSIVE on Qwen (+0.036 [−0.003, +0.084]).

This agrees with R5, which dropped LongBench v2 from the KL claims.

**2. HELMET ICL is easy for everything.** FP scores 1.0 on all 20 units per model; every arm is near FP
in accuracy and NLL.

**3. HELMET rag.** Accuracy is near FP for the system, the floor and Quest on both models. The system's
NLL is worse than Quest's:
- Llama: FAR_FROM_FP, +1.18 [+0.15, +3.11], mostly kilt_nq;
- Qwen: +0.23 [+0.07, +0.41] (SYS_VS_QUEST_HURTS +0.22).

**4. HELMET re-ranking: the system fails on both models, and the vote is why.**

The system's NDCG@10 change against FP:
- Llama: −0.166 [−0.280, −0.060];
- Qwen: −0.261 [−0.410, −0.121], where every vote-read arm scores ≈ 0.005 against FP's 0.266.

Quest at the same 1/8 stays near FP (Qwen +0.018, Llama −0.085). The loss split for Qwen 128K
re-ranking (`R5_0_split.md`), in nats over the answer span, against Llama 128K's:

| part | Qwen 128K | Llama 128K |
|---|---|---|
| budget (exact oracle 1/8 − FP) | +0.77 | +0.48 |
| vote (vote with exact keys − oracle) | +39.8 | +28.6 |
| store (system − vote with exact keys) | −2.7 | −0.5 |

On kilt_nq the vote is again the larger part (Qwen +0.37 against a budget of +0.25; Llama +1.87
against +0.50).

**Qwen's exact oracle at 1/8 still loses NDCG.** It scores −0.129 [−0.263, +0.006]. On this task an
eviction budget of 1/8 costs accuracy by itself. The tail design does not hit this, because it evicts
nothing.

**5. Qwen 128K shows the same split as Llama 128K.** The store adds nothing, the budget is small, and
the vote is the failure. This is the condition plan.md set for Qwen 128K to join R6 ("after R4's Qwen
cells, if they show the same split").
