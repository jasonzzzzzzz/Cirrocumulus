# R5 — what limits the design, and what fixes it (read 2026-10-08)

Rules: `plan.md`, amendment "R5" and "R5 freeze" (frozen before any R5 cell's output).
Exploratory: no labels. Numbers: per span token, unit-clustered bootstrap 90% intervals, each
model × cell × task family separately. Full tables: `R5_reader.md` / `.json` (reader
`read_stage1h_r5.py --r5`); R5.0's split: `R5_0_split.md`; the pilot: `R5_pilot.md`.

## Runs

11 blocks on Trillium (1059655–1059665), 1 GPU each, 15 min – 1 h 56 min, peak 48–65 GiB:
Llama-3.1-8B at 128K (RULER, harder RULER, cwe/fwe, HELMET, LongBench v2) and Qwen3-30B-A3B at
32K (RULER, harder RULER, cwe/fwe). 225 units read.

**Validity (V).** Every bound held on every measured head and step (Lemmas 1 and 3, the
certificate, the score bound); the controller emulation never left its target on any trace (both
bounds, both targets); injected missed mass stayed at or below its target and reached it. One
block fails V and is excluded by the frozen rule: LongBench v2 (1059661), where the probe's pass
differs from FP's on 4 of 20 single-token answers (KL 0.001–0.002, the size of `fp_noise` there;
the forced choice is unchanged on all 20). Every multi-token block has probe KL exactly 0 (360
passes); LongBench v2 is the only cell whose answer runs through the decode path, so this reads as
bf16 nondeterminism, not the probe altering outputs. The block is in the reader's appendix.

## Findings

**1. The tail design removes the vote's failure.** `qread2t4kqT_v4` — the system's selection and
tiers (exact keys on the vote's 1/8 of rows, everything at 4 bits), but nothing evicted — has KL
per token 0.0000–0.011 in all 17 model × cell × family groups, against FP8's 0.0000–0.008; it beats
dense 4-bit and KIVI-4 in most groups, and on every family where the system fails it removes the
loss (KL/token: Qwen harder multivalue 0.0018 vs the system's 0.264; Llama re-ranking 0.011 vs 0.502;
Qwen aggregation 0.0029 vs 0.176; Llama aggregation 0.0009 vs 0.047). Accuracy on FP-correct units is
unchanged in 13 of 16 groups (re-ranking has no FP-correct unit). The exceptions: Llama
aggregation −0.33 (few FP-correct units; FP8 and the exact oracle unchanged, `fp_noise` itself
−0.13), Qwen tracking −0.012 (FP8 −0.006, KIVI-4 −0.006) and Qwen harder multivalue −0.005. Traffic 0.30 of FP16 K+V per decode step (FP8: 0.50; the system:
0.08). This is Lemma 3 in practice: the error is the tail's mass times the 4-bit error.

**2. The vote, not the budget, was the system's problem (confirms R5.0).** The static oracle at the
same 1/8 is near FP almost everywhere; Quest at a matched budget is worse than the system on
Llama's multi-step answers (KL/token 0.02–0.14 on multivalue and tracking).

**3. The tail design's error sits at FP8's level, not below it.** Its relative attention-output
error is 0.04–0.05 per head (FP8: 0.03–0.05), within FP8's on 33–50% of head-steps. The reason is in
the design, not the selection: it reads 4-bit values on the selected rows too, an additive error
that selection cannot remove.

**4. Certifying is the open problem.** Lemma 3's bound at the vote's rows is never within FP8's
error (0% of head-steps): it uses the worst value norm and the worst key error over the tail.
For eviction (Mode E) the certificate is close at tight targets (certified budget 2–3.7× the
oracle's at ε = 0.01; 1.4–2.2× with the high-probability bound) but loose at ε = 0.1 (5–67×),
and the emulated controller reads 21–31% of rows (Llama) or 55–70% (Qwen 32K) at ε = 0.1 with the
high-probability bound, fetching on 15–74% of steps.

**5. The theory's links hold where they can be measured.** KL grows with the injected missed mass
(frozen raw slope 0.4–1.0: a bf16 floor — any nonzero change flips elements by an ulp — makes
small ε flat; post hoc, with each unit's KL at ε = 0.003 removed, slopes are 1.2–2.0, Llama 1.2–2.0);
the layer quarters' KL roughly adds up to the all-layer KL for Llama after the same correction
(0.84–1.36×; Qwen multivalue 2.2–4.2×, noisy); concentration forecasts certified budgets (Spearman
0.80–0.97 Llama, 0.54–0.81 Qwen, per head-step). Realized missed mass and relative output error
explain KL equally (R² 0.15–0.68; the two are 0.98-correlated, so neither adds to the other).

## What this means for the design

- **Read the tail, don't evict it.** With nothing evicted, which rows are exact matters far less
  (Lemma 3: error ∝ tail mass × tier-1 error) — the vote no longer has to be right, only good.
- **Exact values on the selected rows** should remove finding 3's additive term (`qread2t4qT`:
  keys and values from tier 2), putting the error below FP8's.
- **The tail can be coarser than a dense cache.** Its error is multiplied by its mass (≈ 0.05 per
  head here), so 3- or 2-bit tail keys and values may stay near FP while cutting traffic below
  dense 4-bit's 0.25.
- **The certificate should bound Mode T's error with per-row value errors and the tail's actual
  (mass-weighted) value spread**, not worst cases; R5's head records show how far the current bound is.

These are R5.3's arms (plan.md, amendment "R5.3", draft).
