## What R5.4 decides (the frozen rule)

*The rule:* R6's arm becomes the unbiased randomized tail "if Q2b ≤ 0 everywhere, with no accuracy
loss".

- **Read literally, the rule is not met.** Three of ten Q2b point estimates are above 0 (at most
  +0.0006, none with an interval above 0), and Llama tracking's all-unit accuracy is −0.046 (from
  FP-failed units). Under the rule, `tail4x_v4` stays R6's arm.
- **The rule does not cover R6's power.** On these units `tail4x_v4` cannot pass Qwen multivalue at
  R6's margin, and it needs about 110 units for Llama re-ranking. `dtail4ux_v4` passes both at the
  same traffic, and `dtail6ux_v4` needs the fewest units. Switching means overriding a frozen rule
  after seeing its data. That decision is the user's; see plan.md "R5.4 and R4 read".
- **Can R6 carry Theorem R's certificate?** Only with a dithered arm: Theorem R says nothing about
  the Lloyd-Max tier.
  - At 6 bits on the vote's rows, it certifies FP8-level error on about half the head-steps (Llama
    0.44–0.52, Qwen 0.38–0.41).
  - At 8 bits it certifies 0.65–0.79.
  - With 0 misses on 31 million checks, it can be reported as a validity column. It cannot be a
    per-step guarantee at FP8 level without reading more rows (+C/32 gives ≥ 0.98 on Llama).
